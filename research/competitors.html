▶1. OmazeUnited Kingdom (prim
https://omaze.co.uk (UK
A TV-advertised charity prize-draw giant that sells sub-10-pound "entries" to win multi-million-pound houses (plus cars and cash), keeps roughly 83% of revenue, guarantees a headline sum to one charity partner, and quietly converts mass-market dreamers into monthly auto-renewing subscribers.
Модель

Charity prize draw, deliberately structured NOT as a lottery. The legal engine is a free postal / email "no purchase necessary" entry route, which keeps Omaze outside UK Gambling Commission licensing, avoids the National Lottery's 12% duty, and sidesteps the ~20% minimum-to-charity rule that binds licensed society/charity lotteries. Layered on top: a recurring subscription model (monthly auto-renewing entry bundles) plus a separate cash "Monthly Millionaire" draw bundled into subscriptions of 15 pounds and above. Prizes are houses + cars + cash; winners almost always take the cash-equivalent option.

Механіки
  • Guaranteed grand prize: exactly one house winner every monthly draw (no rollover ambiguity), reset each calendar month to create urgency and a fresh 'launch' event.
  • Guaranteed charity floor: a fixed minimum donation (e.g. '1,000,000 pounds to charity no matter what') per major draw — de-risks the promise and gives PR a clean headline number regardless of sales.
  • Tiered entry bundles with declining cost-per-entry (10 pounds = 15 entries; larger spends give proportionally more) to push basket size up.
  • Auto-renewing monthly subscription that re-enters you into whichever house draw closes that calendar month — the core retention mechanic.
  • Bonus / stacked draws: subscribers get extra draws, 'Early Bird' prizes and access to the Monthly Millionaire cash draw for free.
  • Early Bird prize: a large secondary prize (e.g. 100,000 pounds + a Land Rover Defender 110) awarded to an entrant who buys before an early cutoff date — pulls demand forward in the sales window.
  • Free-entry Dream List draw: a monthly 10,000-pound cash prize for anyone opted into marketing emails — a low-friction hook that harvests consented contacts.
  • Monthly Millionaire: 1,000,000-pound cash prize paid in 12 equal monthly instalments, one winner every month, bundled into subscriptions of 15 pounds+.
  • Spend caps as responsible-gambling signalling: 500 pounds overall monthly cap, 250 pounds monthly cap on credit-card payments, self-set reductions to 0 pounds.
  • Cash-alternative on the house: winners overwhelmingly take a cash equivalent instead of moving in (New Statesman: 11 of 14 winners sold by 2023), so the 'dream home' is effectively a jackpot.
  • Prize enrichment: stamp duty, legal fees, ~150,000 pounds furnishings and ~250,000 pounds spending money bundled with the house to maximise the 'life-changing' story.
Ціни та чек

Currency GBP. Single purchase: 10 pounds = 15 entries (entry-level). Subscriptions (auto-renew on the 1st): 10 pounds/month = 60 entries; 15 pounds/month = 100 entries (+ Monthly Millionaire access); 25 pounds/month = 200 entries; 50 pounds/month = 640 entries (marketed as best value). First-month discounts (~5 pounds off) commonly offered. Monthly spend cap 500 pounds total, 250 pounds on credit cards. Effective cost-per-entry falls sharply with bundle size, nudging up-spend. AOV not disclosed; ESTIMATE only — the tier design and 500-pound cap imply a meaningful mid-tier (25-50 pounds) subscriber cohort. Free postal / email entry legally must carry equal odds but excludes bonus/early-bird perks.

Призи / подарунки

Headline prize: a fully renovated multi-million-pound house (avg ~2.9m pounds), mortgage-free, with stamp duty, legal fees, ~150k pounds furnishings and ~250k pounds cash bundled in. Winners are offered — and almost always take — a cash alternative instead of the property (New Statesman: 11 of 14 house winners had sold by 2023). Secondary/gift prizes: Early Bird prize (large cash + a car, e.g. 100k pounds + Land Rover Defender 110 or 100k + VW California campervan). Cash draws: Monthly Millionaire (1m pounds paid over 12 months). Free-entry gift: Dream List monthly 10,000-pound cash draw for email-opted-in users. Structure mixes guaranteed (the house, the charity minimum) with probabilistic bonus prizes.

UI / UX

Mobile-first funnel built around a single hero property. The live draw page leads with cinematic house/drone photography and a countdown, a charity-partner logo (e.g. English Heritage, British Heart Foundation), and a 'select your entries' bundle selector as the primary CTA. Entry building is a quick-pick style tile choice (pick a bundle, not individual numbers), with subscription framed as the default 'best value' option (e.g. 50 pounds/month = 640 entries). Heavy use of FOMO widgets: countdown timers, Early Bird cutoff banners, 'already won' badges. Dedicated iOS/Android app (released ~late 2024) with in-app new-house-launch reminders, subscription management, entry-code verification and charity updates — designed to make each monthly 'House Launch' feel like an event drop. Checkout supports card and digital wallets; the free postal route is present but buried in T&C.

Канали залучення

Dominated by mass-reach paid brand advertising: relentless UK TV advertising (so ubiquitous it drew a Grazia opinion piece on 'the endless Omaze ads'), plus heavy paid social (Meta/Facebook & Instagram, YouTube). Charity partners double as an acquisition/PR channel — each partner (British Heart Foundation, NSPCC, Marie Curie, Age UK, MND Association, Anthony Nolan, English Heritage) promotes the draw to its own supporter base and lends trust. Earned media: winner human-interest stories in Daily Mail, BBC, Time Out, local press for every handover. Email/CRM: the free 'Dream List' draw harvests marketing consent and feeds retention. Political/regulatory lobbying (Hanbury Strategy, Labour conference 2024) protects the model rather than acquires users, but shapes the operating environment.

Trust is engineered heavily. VERIFIED: Trustpilot 4.7/5 'Excellent' across ~19,000 UK reviews (2026); iOS App Store 4.8/5 from ~35,000 UK ratings. Membership / утримання

Subscription is the retention engine: monthly auto-renewing entry bundles (10/15/25/50 pounds) that automatically enter the subscriber into each month's house draw, so lapse = losing your standing entry. Retention is reinforced by (a) the sunk-cost 'I've entered for years' narrative Omaze amplifies in winner stories (e.g. 4-year subscriber won), (b) free bonus perks stacked onto subscriptions (Monthly Millionaire, Early Bird access, extra draws), and (c) the free Dream List email draw keeping lapsed/free users in the funnel. The dedicated app pushes 'new House Launch' reminders monthly to re-engage. Friction on the way out: multiple negative app reviews cite difficulty cancelling and repeated charges — a retention red flag / reputational risk.

🔴 Червоні лінії (не копіювати)
  • 'Subscription = draw access': Omaze's core auto-renew model explicitly sells recurring entries. For DreamCar this is a hard red line — a recurring fee whose reward is chance-of-winning is exactly the cash-random-among-payers pattern to avoid.
  • 83% retained / 17% to charity: the opaque revenue split and 'guaranteed minimum' framing drew regulator and press scrutiny (New Statesman, Fundraising Regulator). DreamCar should never obscure where money goes or lean on a charity-veneer it can't substantiate.
  • Bigger-spend-buys-more-chance: declining cost-per-entry bundles that transparently sell MORE odds for MORE money. DreamCar's номер участі must stay a fixed bonus, decoupled from spend tier.
  • Cancellation friction / repeated charges (documented in app reviews) — a reputational and consumer-protection risk DreamCar must not replicate.
  • Marketing the prize as the product: Omaze's 'win a house' hero eclipses any real utility. DreamCar's utility-first defence requires the AI-token PRODUCT, not the car, to be the headline offer.

A free/postal entry route is offered for legal cover.
